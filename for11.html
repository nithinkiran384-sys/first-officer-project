📘 Help & Guide

Welcome! This page will guide you through the complete process of creating an account,
logging in, and accessing real-time project tracking on our platform.

STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE

1 Create an Account (Register)

This is your first step. If you are a new user, you need to register before you can access anything.

  • Go to the main page of our application.
  • You will see two options: Login and Register.
  • Click on "Register" (also called "Sign Up" or "Create Account").
  • Fill in the required details to create a new account.
  • Click "Submit" or "Create Account" to finish registration.
✅ Important: Registration is a one-time process. Once your account is created, you will keep the same username/email and password for future logins.
⬇

2 Login to Your Account

After registering, go back to the main page and this time choose Login.

  • Go back to the main page (the first screen you saw).
  • Click on Login (also called "Sign In").
  • Enter the username/email and password you created during registration.
  • Click "Login" to access your account.
🔐 Tip: Logging in is required every time you visit the app. Make sure you remember your username/email and password. Use the "Forgot Password" link if you forget.
⬇

3 Enter Project Details

Once you are logged in, the app will ask you to fill in some project information. This is required so we can set up your project correctly.

📋 Details You Will Be Asked To Fill:

📁

Project Name

The official name of your project

🔢

Project Code

A unique code/ID for the project

📍

Location

City or area where project is based

🏢

Address

Full address of the project site

✍️ Note: Fill in all the details carefully and accurately. These details are used to identify and track your project throughout its lifecycle.
⬇

4 View Real-Time Project Tracking

After entering your project details, you will be taken to the main dashboard where you can view real-time tracking of your project.

  • See the live status of your project.
  • Track progress, updates and milestones in real time.
  • Monitor everything from your personal dashboard.
📡 Good to know: The real-time tracking updates automatically, so you can always see the latest information about your project without refreshing the page.
